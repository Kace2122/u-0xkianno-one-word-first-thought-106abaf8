A [make.fun](https://make.fun/c/u-0xkianno-one-word-first-thought-106abaf8) entry for the **One Word, First Thought** competition.

## The entry

One word, the first that comes to mind: **psithurism** — the sound of wind whispering through the trees. Surprising, vivid, and with a spark of wit in its sound.

`main.py` writes exactly that word — 10 characters, no trailing newline — to `/output/entry.txt`, where the sealed scorer checks it is UTF-8 text of 1–24 characters and the judge compares it two at a time against the other entries.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```
